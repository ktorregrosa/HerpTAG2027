# Travel Information

## Airports
There are several airports in the area. LaGuardia is likely your best option for ease and the number of direct flights. Westchester Airport is a smaller and easier airport to navigate, but there are fewer flights. Try to avoid flying into JFK, if you can.
- Westchester Airport (HPN)
- John F. Kennedy Airport (JFK)
- LaGuardia Airport (LGA)

  Uber or Taxis are available to the hotel from all airports. It is possible to get to the hotel by (multiple) trains from LaGuardia or JFK.
